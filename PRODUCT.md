# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Security-conscious teams deploying AI workflows. They need to understand, at a glance, how actions are evaluated and how approved activity can be traced.

## Product Purpose

The site presents a security layer for user prompts, tool calls, agent operations, and automations. It screens risky activity and provides signed, immutable audit proof for approved actions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, trustworthy. The site should convey control and clarity rather than alarm or visual spectacle.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon cyber-security aesthetics, noisy dashboard-card compositions, and generic animated AI gradients. Do not make risk messaging feel sensational.

## Design Principles

- Explain the security boundary in one readable glance.
- Let the established violet and blue theme carry identity; use status color sparingly.
- Make proof and traceability feel precise and tangible.
- Prefer restrained diagrams and meaningful details over decorative effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG-friendly contrast in both themes. Do not convey state through color alone, and preserve a legible static experience with reduced motion.
